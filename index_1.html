Published research

Genetic Algorithm-Optimized Lightweight CNN for Low-Resolution Face Recognition

ICTMIM 2026 · presented at Rohini College of Engineering and Technology, Kanyakumari

Low-resolution faces, like those from surveillance cameras, break most recognition models. I designed a small CNN and used a genetic algorithm to search for its architecture and feature-extraction settings. The model learns face embeddings and verifies identity by cosine similarity, so it stays accurate while using less compute. That makes it a fit for real-time and edge devices.
